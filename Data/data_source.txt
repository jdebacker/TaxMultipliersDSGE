--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEA Fixed Assets Table 2.7 (used by 0_pre_prep.py for e_tau weights)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Endpoint: https://apps.bea.gov/api/data/  (DataSetName=FixedAssets)
TableName: FAAt207  -- "Investment in Private Fixed Assets, Equipment,
                       Structures, and Intellectual Property Products by Type"
Coverage: annual, 1901-current

0_pre_prep.py pulls Table 2.7, drops aggregate parent rows, expands annual
to quarterly (each year's value applied to all four quarters), and assigns
0/1 bonus-eligibility flags by MACRS recovery period.

Outputs to raw/:
    PQdat_BEA.csv     quarterly nominal investment by detailed asset type
    treat_BEA.csv     0/1 eligibility vector aligned to PQdat_BEA columns
    asset_types.txt   numbered asset list with eligibility flags

Eligibility (Section 168(k) bonus depreciation):
    Recovery period <= 20 years    -> 1 (eligible)
        equipment, software, R&D, entertainment originals,
        utility/telecom/farm/oil-gas quasi-structures
    Recovery period > 20 years     -> 0 (not eligible)
        commercial structures (39yr), residential (27.5yr), land

Section 179 expensing NOT included (matches House-Shapiro practice).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
House-Shapiro (2008) ITC files (used for tau_itc only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local files in raw/:
    ITCdat.txt   Quarterly ITC rate by asset type (38 cols, 1959Q1-2007Q1)
    PQdat.txt    Quarterly nominal investment by asset type (38 cols)

Used by build_tau_ic in 1_build_data.py to construct the aggregate ITC
rate. PQdat.txt provides type weights; the 38-type taxonomy aligns with
ITCdat.txt's columns. (BEA's Fixed Assets Table 2.7 uses a different
asset taxonomy and so isn't usable for ITC weighting.)

Coverage outside HS sample:
    1958Q1-1958Q4: ITC = 0 (didn't exist), weights backfilled from 1959Q1
    2007Q2-current: ITC = 0 (statutory, repealed 1986), weights forward-filled


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
McGrattan-TAXSIM federal marginal tax rates (used for tau_i and tau_div)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Local file in raw/:
    taxsim_fed_McGrattan.txt   Annual federal marginal tax rates from the
                               NBER TAXSIM model (Feenberg-Coutts 1993),
                               distributed by Ellen McGrattan. Columns
                               include Wages, Interest, Dividends,
                               Qualified Dividends, ST/LT Gains, Pensions,
                               Property Tax, Mortgage Interest, Cash Gifts.
                               Coverage 1960-2013, annual.

Used by build_taxsim_rates in 1_build_data.py to construct:
    tau_i        Interest income tax rate (Interest column / 100)
    tau_div      Dividend income tax rate (Qualified Dividends column / 100)

The Qualified Dividends column is used as the dividend-tax measure from the McGrattan-TAXSIM file. 
Around the JGTRRA period, it captures the preferential tax treatment of qualified dividends. 
Before qualified-dividend treatment is relevant, the series should be interpreted as the TAXSIM dividend-income marginal tax rate.

Coverage outside 1960-2013:
    1958Q1-1959Q4: NaN
    2014Q1-current: NaN


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Constructed series and transformations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real per-capita levels (chat, ihat, ghat, bhat, lhat, trhat):
    100 * (log(real / pop) - linear trend)
    nominal / (GDPDEF/100), divided by population in persons

pi  = GDPDEF[t] / GDPDEF[t-1]            (gross inflation ratio)
r   = FEDFUNDS / 400                     (quarterly decimal rate)

Tax rates (Jones 2002 / Mendoza-Razin-Tesar 1994):
    CI    = Rental + CP + NI + PRI/2
    tau_p = (FIT + SIT) / (W + PRI/2 + CI)
    tau_l = (tau_p*(W + PRI/2) + CSI) / (EC + PRI/2)
    tau_k = (tau_p*CI + CT_fed + CT_sl + PT) / CI                 (Jones 2002)
    tau_c = (TPI - PT) / (PCE_total + GCE - (TPI - PT))
    tau_d = tau_p                        (proxy)

Each detrendable tax rate is exposed as TWO hat variants in US_Data_Full:
    tau_x_hat_detrend     -- (linear-detrended - mean) / mean
    tau_x_hat_nodetrend   -- (level - mean) / mean

3_clean_for_matlab.py picks one per tax via HAT_CHOICE and renames it to
the canonical tau_x_hat for the .mod file.

tau_itc:
    Federal Investment Tax Credit. Existed 1962-1986; repealed by the
    Tax Reform Act of 1986. Quarterly rates by asset type from House-
    Shapiro's ITCdat.txt (1959Q1-2007Q1), backfilled with zero for 1958
    (pre-existence) and post-2007Q1 (statutory zero). Aggregated using
    HS's own PQdat.txt for type weights (since the 38-type ITC rates
    align with HS's 38-type investment shares). LEVEL deviation:
    tau_itc_hat = tau_itc - mean(tau_itc).

e_tau (aggregate bonus-depreciation expensing share):
    e_tau[t] = bonus[t] * eligible_share[t]
    where eligible_share[t] = sum_m (PQ_BEA[t,m] / sum_j PQ_BEA[t,j]) * treat[m]
    Uses LEVEL deviation: e_tau_hat = e_tau - mean(e_tau).
    Set etau_ss in the .mod file to mean(e_tau) over the estimation window.

tau_i and tau_div (interest and dividend tax rates):
    Annual federal marginal tax rates from McGrattan-TAXSIM file,
    expanded to quarterly by applying each year's value to all four
    quarters. Pre-1960 and post-2013 are NaN.
    Each series gets two variants (parallel to tau_l/tau_k/tau_c/tau_d):
        tau_x_hat_detrend    = linear-detrended fractional deviation
                               (trend fit on observed quarters only)
        tau_x_hat_nodetrend  = (tau_x - mean) / mean


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bonus depreciation calendar (hard-coded in 1_build_data.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Pre-2001Q4:    0     (no bonus regime)
    2001Q4-2003Q2: 30%   JCWAA 2002 (retroactive to 9/11/2001)
    2003Q3-2004Q4: 50%   JGTRRA 2003
    2005Q1-2007Q4: 0     bonus expired
    2008Q1-2010Q4: 50%   ESA 2008 + ARRA 2009 + SBJA 2010
    2011Q1-2011Q4: 100%  Tax Relief Act 2010
    2012Q1-2013Q4: 50%   ATRA 2012
    2014Q1-2017Q3: 50%   TIPA 2014 + PATH 2015
    2017Q4-2022Q4: 100%  TCJA 2017
    2023Q1-2023Q4: 80%   TCJA phase-down
    2024Q1-2024Q4: 60%
    2025Q1:        40%   pre-OBBBA effective date
    2025Q2+:       100%  OBBBA 2025 (permanent)
